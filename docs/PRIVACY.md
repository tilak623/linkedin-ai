# Privacy Policy & Privacy Centre Specification

## 1. User Data Ownership Principles

In the **Personal Branding AI Agent**, you retain complete, unequivocal ownership over all your professional data, learning records, and interview transcripts.

- **Your Data Stays Yours**: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your professional data.
- **Local-First Processing**: When offline, data resides entirely within your local SQLite file (`brand_agent.db`) on your own laptop.
- **No Unsolicited Scraping**: The app does not scrape your private browser history or private chats.

---

## 2. Privacy Centre Capabilities

The dedicated **Settings & Privacy Centre** in the UI grants users full control over their stored memory:

### 2.1 View & Search Stored Records
- Browse all accumulated skills, categorized into *implemented*, *studied*, *explored*, and *discovered*.
- Review every past interview session and extracted insight.
- Inspect all published and unpublished post drafts.

### 2.2 Rectification & Correction
- Edit, amend, or downgrade skill proficiency levels at any time.
- Correct outdated project descriptions or erroneous technical details.

### 2.3 One-Click Data Export
- Export complete profile, memory, interview transcripts, and post histories in machine-readable JSON or human-readable Markdown format.

### 2.4 Complete Data Deletion (Right to Be Forgotten)
- Delete individual interview turns, memory entries, or posts.
- Trigger complete database purge to wipe all records from both local SQLite and cloud Supabase tables.

### 2.5 Integration Disconnect
- Revoke GitHub connection and unlink repositories with a single click.
- Revoke LinkedIn OAuth permissions and clear cached member tokens instantly.
